# B2B SaaS GTM Analytics & Sales Strategy Dashboard

An executive Tableau dashboard designed to analyze revenue performance, pipeline health, forecasting, territory coverage, sales performance, and customer retention for a synthetic B2B SaaS organization.

---

## Project Overview

This project simulates a B2B SaaS sales organization and brings key Go-to-Market (GTM) and sales strategy metrics into a single executive dashboard.

The dashboard was designed to provide a consolidated view of revenue performance, pipeline health, forecast accuracy, territory coverage, AE performance, and Net Revenue Retention (NRR).

The goal was to create an executive-level analytics experience that helps sales and GTM leaders identify performance trends, coverage gaps, forecasting risks, and opportunities for growth.

The dashboard includes six core analytical views:

- **Revenue Attainment vs Quota**  
  Tracks quarterly revenue performance against quota and highlights contribution by customer segment.

- **Pipeline Stage Distribution**  
  Analyzes deal volume and ACV across the sales funnel to identify pipeline concentration and progression.

- **Forecast Intelligence**  
  Compares quota, commit forecast, actual closed revenue, and best-case performance across regions.

- **Territory Coverage Gap**  
  Identifies territories with insufficient pipeline coverage and highlights areas requiring additional sales capacity or pipeline generation.

- **AE Performance Quadrant**  
  Evaluates sales representatives based on attainment and win rate while incorporating ramp status and quota performance.

- **NRR Waterfall**  
  Breaks down ARR movement across expansion, contraction, and churn to understand customer retention and revenue growth.

## Key Business Insights

The dashboard was designed to surface several executive-level GTM insights:

### Revenue Performance

Quarterly revenue attainment provides a view of how actual performance is tracking against quota while showing how different customer segments contribute to overall revenue.

### Pipeline Health

Pipeline stage analysis highlights where deals are concentrated within the funnel and provides visibility into both deal volume and potential ACV.

### Forecast Risk

Comparing commit, actual closed revenue, quota, and best-case scenarios helps identify regions where forecasted performance may diverge from target expectations.

### Territory Coverage

Territory-level pipeline coverage highlights areas below the desired coverage threshold and distinguishes between:

- **Gap**
- **Watch**
- **On Track**

This provides a framework for identifying territories that may require additional pipeline generation or sales capacity.

### Sales Rep Performance

The AE performance view combines attainment and win rate to identify different performance patterns across the sales organization and distinguish ramping representatives from fully ramped representatives.

### Customer Retention & Expansion

The NRR waterfall provides visibility into how expansion, contraction, and churn contribute to changes in recurring revenue across customer cohorts.

## Data

The project uses synthetic data created specifically for portfolio and demonstration purposes.

The datasets represent different components of a B2B SaaS sales organization, including:

- Quarterly revenue and quota performance
- Pipeline and deal stages
- Regional forecast and commit performance
- AE performance
- Customer cohort ARR movement
- Territory pipeline coverage

No real customer, employee, company, or financial data is used in this project.

## Business Strategy Framework

The dashboard brings together several areas of GTM and sales strategy into one analytical framework:

| Area | Key Metrics |
|---|---|
| Revenue Performance | Revenue, Quota, Attainment |
| Pipeline Management | Deal Count, ACV, Stage Conversion |
| Forecasting | Commit, Actual Closed, Best Case |
| Territory Planning | Pipeline Coverage, Coverage Rate, Headcount |
| Sales Performance | Attainment, Win Rate, Quota |
| Customer Retention | Expansion, Contraction, Churn, NRR |

This structure is intended to demonstrate how operational sales data can be translated into an executive-level view of GTM performance.

## Project Purpose

This project demonstrates the use of data visualization and business analytics to translate sales and GTM data into actionable insights for executive decision-making.

The focus was not only on building individual charts, but on creating a cohesive analytical experience that connects revenue performance, pipeline, forecasting, sales capacity, rep performance, and customer retention.
